# Independent ETC5543 examiner review

Review date: 28 October 2026. Assessed artefact: the locally regenerated 24-page `report/final_report.pdf`, its Quarto source and the project working tree. This is a provisional independent assessment, not an actual lecturer grade.

## Overall judgement

No fabricated core statistical result was found. Independent recomputation from the retained raw files reproduced the main row counts, date range, Noise distribution, site medians, hourly extrema, duplicate counts, pedestrian sample and correlation, and hospitality screen and correlation. The strongest analytical work is the cautious treatment of an undocumented measurement field, unequal coverage, small spatial sample and sensitivity to aggregation and buffer choice.

The report is not presently in the 90--100 band. It is polished and technically substantial, but the official description for that band requires an outstanding, concise, flowing report with profound understanding, clear justification and few if any improvements. Material improvements remain: the final reproduction package is uncommitted, the Python preflight is not clean, individual contributions still require human confirmation, the literature base is narrow for a report of this length, and the primary spatial scale has only pragmatic rather than literature-based justification.

On the revised local version, a defensible provisional range is **78--82**. The lower end reflects unresolved submission and contribution evidence; the upper end reflects strong data auditing, restrained interpretation and unusually transparent sensitivity analysis. Before the verified corrections in this review, a range around **74--79** would have been more appropriate.

## Statistical and citation audit conclusions

- **Unsupported or fabricated findings:** none confirmed among the headline numerical results. Raw-file recomputation reproduced 975,550 rows, 893,959 finite Noise values, the 0.0--131.1 range, site medians 52.3--81.1, hourly extrema, 2,745 repeated timestamp groups, pedestrian rho 0.248 at n=10, and hospitality rho 0.727 at n=11.
- **Incorrect interpretation found:** the hospitality source sum was not a cross-source unique-venue count. This is corrected and sensitivity-tested.
- **References:** no fabricated source was found. Bibliographic details and report claims were checked at varying depths recorded in the revised citation audit. The main weakness is breadth and methodological relevance, not obvious fabrication.
- **Cross-references/rendering:** all figure/table references resolve; no raw Quarto cross-reference token remains; the final PDF has 24 pages and was visually inspected page by page.
- **Word count:** compliant, with a conservative narrative count of 10,257 excluding references.

## Performance-band comparison

### 90--100: not met

The work shows strong judgement, but it is not “outstanding in all respects” and still has several clear improvements. In particular, repository completeness, exact Python environment reproduction, contribution verification, literature breadth and buffer justification remain below the descriptor.

### 80--89: partly met

The report is organised, mostly easy to follow and demonstrates excellent understanding of data limitations. It contains several strong insights: the measurement-unit boundary, pooled versus equal-sensor comparisons, stability diagnostics, retention of negative results and the cross-source union check. The unresolved reproducibility and contribution evidence, together with thin methodological literature and some repetition, keep the likely result near the lower edge of this band rather than its middle or top.

### 70--79: securely met

The report is coherent, well written and technically competent, with only a limited number of substantive analytical shortcomings after correction. It demonstrates very good understanding and several good insights, but has clear areas for improvement. This descriptor closely matches the lower half of the provisional range.
