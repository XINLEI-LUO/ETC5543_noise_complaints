# RQ3 Hospitality Follow-up Summary

This report is generated by `analysis/rq3_hospitality_followup.py`.

## Analysis status

This is an exploratory continuous follow-up conducted after the frozen median-based RQ3 screen returned no candidate at the primary 500 m buffer. It does not replace or alter that screen.

## Result

At 500 m, the Spearman association between lagged 2024 hospitality venue count and 2025 night-time recorded Noise is 0.727 (two-sided 99,999-draw permutation p = 0.014; n = 11). The leave-one-site-out range is 0.685 to 0.782.

Buffer results are:

- 250 m: rho = 0.699; permutation p = 0.021; leave-one-site-out range = 0.659 to 0.781
- 500 m: rho = 0.727; permutation p = 0.014; leave-one-site-out range = 0.685 to 0.782
- 1,000 m: rho = 0.536; permutation p = 0.095; leave-one-site-out range = 0.442 to 0.685

The positive direction is retained across all three buffers, while the magnitude is weaker at 1,000 m. These descriptive results apply only to the monitored locations. They do not show that hospitality venues caused higher recorded values, and the 2024 venue counts are a lagged context rather than a contemporaneous 2025 exposure measure.
