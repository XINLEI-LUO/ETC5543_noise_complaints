## Duplicate sensitivity

The source has no exact duplicate 2024 rows. One three-row group shares trading name, address, ANZSIC4 and coordinates but has different property IDs. The primary features retain these source records. A sensitivity dataset keeps one record for that composite key. The group is outside all analysed sensor buffers, producing zero differences in active-business counts and normalised entropy across all 33 sensor-buffer rows.

## Decision

The CLUE extension is suitable for descriptive and carefully limited exploratory analysis. The primary source-preserving version is retained because the ambiguous candidate records do not affect any sensor-buffer feature, and deleting them would impose an unsupported establishment-identity assumption. The existing validated noise and urban-activity outputs remain unchanged.
